3. Who receives your data

  • Charging station partners operating the station you use, limited to what is needed for that session.
  • Service providers acting on our instructions: hosting, email and SMS delivery, payment processing.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside Morocco. Such transfers are made only with the authorization of the CNDP or to countries offering an adequate level of protection, with appropriate safeguards.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account data: as long as your account is active. When you delete your account it is disabled immediately and anonymized after a 14 day grace period.
  • Charging sessions, orders and invoices: kept for the period required by Moroccan accounting and tax law, detached from your identity once your account is deleted.
  • Technical charger logs: 90 days. Read notifications: 180 days.
  • Data export archives: 7 days, then deleted.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data and receive a copy (Profile > Privacy > Request a copy of my data);
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Profile > Personal information);
  • Erase your data (Profile > Privacy > Delete my account);
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Data portability: the export is provided in a machine-readable format (JSON).

You can also write to {{ legal.contactEmail }}. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can lodge a complaint with the CNDP (www.cndp.ma).

6. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, traffic is encrypted, access to personal data is limited to staff who need it and every administrative change is recorded in an audit log.

7. Changes

We will tell you about material changes to this policy by email or in the application before they take effect.
