# Stored and compared instants are UTC; business calendar days come from IBusinessClock.
P:System.DateTime.Now;Use DateTime.UtcNow, or IBusinessClock.LocalNow for business wall-clock time
P:System.DateTime.Today;Use IBusinessClock.Today (business date) and IBusinessClock.StartOfDayUtc
P:System.DateTimeOffset.Now;Use DateTimeOffset.UtcNow
